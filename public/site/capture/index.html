Capture Everything. Understand Every Document.

A paper document is prepared, sorted by category and scanned. Poor scans are rescanned, then ECM+ reads the text in Arabic and English and proposes the index fields for staff to confirm. The digital copy is archived with a barcode or QR code, and the paper original is stored under the same code.

            02Capture channels

            One Platform. Multiple Ways to Capture

            • Scanning

              The local scanner service scans straight into the archive, page by page or through the auto-feeder.

            • Digital upload

              Upload from a device, start from a template or a blank document, or capture from Google Drive and OneDrive.

            • Email capture

              Gmail, Outlook or a business mailbox connects to the suite; emails are filed into a correspondence or the archive, and the Outlook add-in saves and routes them.

            • External system integration

              A REST API lets other systems file documents into the archive, and existing archives are migrated in.

            • Camera capture

              Photographing a document from a phone or tablet and filing it like a scan.

            03Document intelligence

            Beyond Digitization. Into Document Intelligence.

            • Image processing

              Straightening, cropping and cleaning scanned pages before recognition.

            • Text recognition, Arabic and English

              OCR reads scanned and image files, so their content is searchable and opens at the matching page.

            • Classification

              Suggesting the document type from its content. In ECM+ today, staff choose the type, and its index fields follow.

            • Metadata extraction

              Auto fill from file reads the document and proposes values for the type’s index fields: text, numbers and dates. Only empty fields are filled, and staff review before saving. An invoice yields its number, date and total; a contract its parties and expiry; an employee file its name, number and hire date.

            • Barcode and QR codes

              ECM+ generates a code for each document and stamps it on the pages.

            • Validation

              Checking proposed values against rules, such as a due date after the invoice date.

            04Benefits

            Turn Document Processing into Business Efficiency

            • Less manual entry

              Index fields are proposed from the file, so staff confirm rather than type.

            • Searchable

              Recognized text and index fields make every captured document findable by meaning and by field.

            • One archive for every channel

              Paper, files, cloud drives and email land in the same document types and folders.

            • Governed from capture

              Folder permissions, versions and the audit trail apply the moment a document is saved.

            05Advanced capabilities

            Built for high-volume, bilingual archives

            • Auto-feeder scanning
            • Audio and video files
            • Archive migration from existing systems
            • REST API for other systems
            • Google Drive and OneDrive capture
            • Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint add-ins that save and route emails and files
            • Generated barcode and QR stamps
            • Batch splitting by barcode

            06By department

            The same capture, in every department

            • Finance

              Supplier invoices scanned in batches; invoice number, date and total proposed for review.

            • Human Resources

              Employee files captured in Arabic, with name, number and hire date proposed, under HR-only folder permissions.

            • Procurement

              Quotations and purchase orders arriving by email are filed from the inbox or the Outlook add-in.

            • Legal

              Contracts uploaded with parties, reference and expiry date proposed; retention rules can send scheduled notifications.

            • Administration

              Incoming letters scanned and filed into correspondence, with their serial number and due date.
