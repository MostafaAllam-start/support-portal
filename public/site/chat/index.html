01The challenge

Knowledge Is Trapped in Documents

Organizations accumulate thousands of documents that hold important information, yet most of it can only be reached by opening files one at a time.

  • Finding information means opening documents

    Employees search by file name, then read through lengthy files by hand.

  • Time goes into searching

    Valuable working hours are spent looking for answers instead of using them.

  • Information is spread out

    Documents sit across departments and repositories, with no single view.

  • Relationships stay hidden

    It is hard to see how a contract, its invoices and its appendices connect.

06Under the hood

The Architecture in One Line of Sight

A small chain of well-understood steps turns a document repository into something you can question.

  1. 1ECM+ document repository
  2. 2OCR and document processing
  3. 3Text extraction and chunking
  4. 4Embedding generation
  5. 5Vector / semantic indexing
  6. 6User question
  7. 7Relevant context retrieval
  8. 8LLM / RAG response generation
  9. 9Answer with document citations
OCR
Converts scanned document images into machine-readable text.
Chunking
Breaks lengthy documents into smaller, meaningful sections.
Embeddings
Represent content in a form that allows similarity searches by meaning.
Vector indexing
Organizes those representations so the right passages are found quickly.
RAG
Retrieves relevant passages from your documents and gives them to the AI model as context before it answers.
LLM
Understands the question and writes a natural language answer from the retrieved context.

The AI model does not have to be retrained from scratch each time a document is added. New or updated documents are processed and indexed into the knowledge base.

09Security and governance

Intelligence That Respects Your Permissions

Document intelligence only works if it follows the rules your organization already has. A person never receives an answer built from a document they could not open themselves.

  • Existing access permissions

    The assistant works within the permissions already set on documents and folders.

  • Permission-aware retrieval

    Passages from documents a user cannot access are never retrieved for them.

  • Controlled sensitive information

    Sensitive content stays behind the same controls that protect it today.

  • Source traceability

    Every answer links back to the documents and pages it relied on.

  • Organizational data governance

    Knowledge stays inside the governance framework that already applies to your records.
