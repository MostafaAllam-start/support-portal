About ECM Plus

ECM Plus is the Canadian company behind the ECM+ Business Suite.

ECM Plus owns and publishes the ECM+ Business Suite, a unified suite for the management of documents, correspondence, processes and meetings.

Request a demoExplore the suite

01Who we are

A Canadian company and the owner of the suite

ECM Plus is a Canadian company and the owner and publisher of the ECM+ Business Suite.

The suite brings document management, official correspondence, process automation and meeting management together in one bilingual environment with a single sign-in.

02The suite

4 integrated modules with a single sign-in

  • DMSDocument ManagementCapture, classify, version and retrieve every document.
  • CMSCorrespondence ManagementRegister, route, approve and dispatch official correspondence.
  • BPMBusiness Process ManagementDesign processes, build forms and run them with deadlines.
  • MMSMeeting ManagementRun committees from agenda to signed minutes.

03Approach

How the suite serves organizations

  • On your organization’s servers

    ECM+ runs on your organization’s servers, in your own data centre or in the cloud, so your documents remain under your control.

  • Arabic and English

    Complete Arabic and English interfaces with right-to-left layouts, and the Hijri and Gregorian calendars.

  • Security in every module

    An audit trail, access trace codes, two-factor authentication, Nafath sign-in and digital signatures.

  • Licensed by module

    Each module is licensed separately.

  • Your existing archive

    Structured imports bring legacy archives into ECM+. They resume where they stopped and can be rolled back.

  • Connected to your systems

    Other systems connect securely, and each connection is limited to the document types and folders you grant.
