02Lifecycle

The suite governs each record from capture to retention.

Information in ECM+ moves through six stages: capture, registration, routing, approval, archiving and retention. Each step is recorded in the audit trail, from receipt of the file to the application of its retention rules.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Files are captured from devices, scanners, templates, Google Drive or OneDrive, or as voice and video notes. Text recognition reads scanned files, and AI fills each document's index fields for review.

    • DMS
  2. 02

    Register

    Incoming, outgoing and internal correspondence receives a serial number, parties, priority, sensitivity and a due date.

    • CMS
  3. 03

    Route

    Correspondence is routed to employees, groups, departments or external parties, and overdue process steps escalate automatically.

    • CMS
    • BPM
  4. 04

    Approve

    Correspondence is reviewed and then approved or returned, and committees circulate their minutes for digital signature.

    • CMS
    • MMS
  5. 05

    Archive

    Documents are filed with 9 types of access permissions, typed metadata and versions that can be restored.

    • DMS
  6. 06

    Retain

    Retention rules schedule deletion or notification actions, and the audit trail records every view and download.

    • DMS

03Security

Every view and download is traceable.

Each protected view or download carries a unique access trace code. Scanning the code on a leaked page identifies the user, the time, the channel, the device and the network address.

  • Audit trail

    Every addition, view, edit, download, print, share, deletion and lock is recorded.

  • Two-factor authentication and Nafath

    Verification codes are sent by WhatsApp or email. Nafath sign-in uses the Saudi national identity, alone or together with a password.

  • Digital signatures

    Employees sign digitally, and forms and minutes accept drawn or saved signatures.

  • Backups

    Scheduled backups and named snapshots can be restored, and old copies are removed on schedule.

05Sectors

How the suite applies to each sector.

The following example scenarios illustrate the capabilities of the suite. They are not customer case studies.

  • Government and municipalities

    Serial-numbered correspondence passes through review before external dispatch. Staff sign in with Nafath, and committees conclude with signed minutes.

  • Banking and finance

    Retention rules run on schedule, and the forensic watermark traces each protected view. Approvals follow defined deadlines, and the audit trail is complete.

  • Healthcare

    Structured records hold the required fields, and sensitive files remain locked behind a one-time verification code. Every download carries an access trace code.

  • Education

    Committees meet, vote and sign their minutes. Their archive retrieves any document down to the page.

  • Energy, engineering and construction

    Tender archives keep every version, and documents are edited in Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Legacy archives are migrated through structured imports.

Frequently asked questions

Can ECM+ run on our own servers?

Yes. ECM+ is installed on your organization’s servers, in your own data centre or in the cloud, so your documents remain under your control.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Each module is licensed separately, and the other modules remain inactive until required.

Can we bring our existing archive?

Yes. You can move your existing archive from legacy systems into ECM+ through structured imports. If an import is interrupted, it resumes from where it stopped, and it can be rolled back if needed.
