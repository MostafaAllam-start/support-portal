04Lifecycle

The suite governs each record from capture to retention.

Information in ECM+ moves through six stages: capture, registration, routing, approval, archiving and retention. Each step is recorded in the audit trail, from receipt of the file to the application of its retention rules.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Files are captured from devices, scanners, templates, Google Drive or OneDrive, or as voice and video notes. Text recognition reads scanned files, and AI fills each document's index fields for review.

    • DMS
  2. 02

    Register

    Incoming, outgoing and internal correspondence receives a serial number, its sender and recipient, a priority, a sensitivity level and a due date.

    • CMS
  3. 03

    Route

    Correspondence is routed to employees, groups, departments or external parties, and process tasks move between their assignees with every step monitored.

    • CMS
    • BPM
  4. 04

    Approve

    Correspondence is reviewed and then approved or returned, and committees circulate their minutes for digital signature to the members the meeting organizer selects.

    • CMS
    • MMS
  5. 05

    Archive

    Documents are filed with 9 types of access permissions, typed metadata and file versions that can be restored.

    • DMS
  6. 06

    Retain

    Retention rules schedule deletion or notification actions, and the audit trail records every view and download.

    • DMS

05Security

Every view and download is traceable.

Each protected view or download carries a unique access trace code. Scanning the code on a leaked page identifies the user, the time, the channel, the device and the network address.

  • Audit trail

    Every addition, view, edit, download, print, share and deletion is recorded.

  • Two-step verification and document locking

    Users verify their identity in two steps with a code sent to WhatsApp or email, and sensitive documents can be locked so they open only with a one-time code.

  • Digital signatures

    Employees sign digitally, and forms and minutes accept saved signatures.

  • Backups

    Scheduled backups and named restore points can be recovered whenever needed, and older copies are removed automatically on a set schedule.

08Getting started

From the first module to the whole suite, at your pace.

ECM+ is adopted in stages: one module to begin with, your archive brought across, your systems connected, and further modules when you are ready.

  1. 01

    Choose where to begin

    Start with one module or several. The others stay visible in the suite and switch on when you license them.

  2. 02

    Install on your servers

    ECM+ is set up in your own data centre or your cloud, and users sign in with two-step verification or Nafath.

  3. 03

    Bring your archive

    Paper and electronic archives move in structured imports that resume where they stopped and can be rolled back. Scanned files get text recognition and indexing, and completeness is verified before go-live.

  4. 04

    Connect your systems

    Teams, Outlook, Gmail, Google Drive, OneDrive and WhatsApp connect to the suite, and your own systems and databases connect through the REST API.

  5. 05

    Add modules as you grow

    License the next module when you need it, under the same sign-in. Your teams reach ECM+ support through the client portal, in Arabic or English.

Frequently asked questions

Can ECM+ run on our own servers?

Yes. ECM+ is installed on your organization’s servers, in your own data centre or in the cloud, so your documents remain under your control.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. You can start with one module, and the others remain inactive until required.

Can we bring our existing archive?

Yes. You can move your existing archive from legacy systems into ECM+ through structured imports. If an import is interrupted, it resumes from where it stopped, and it can be rolled back if needed.
